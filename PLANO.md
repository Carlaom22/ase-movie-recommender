## Summary

All team members contributed to both backend and frontend development, ensuring shared technical understanding and avoiding isolated development silos. Each member assumed responsibility for a specific functional focus area—authentication, movies, ratings, or integration—while actively collaborating across components.

This balanced and collaborative development approach improved system coherence, facilitated knowledge sharing, and contributed to the successful delivery of the project within the scope and objectives of the ASE course unit.
